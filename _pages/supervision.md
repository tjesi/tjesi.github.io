## Internships or Research Visits

I am happy to host internship students interested in my area of research, but I do not have funding for this myself. You need to apply for a scholarship, for example from [Erasmus+](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu) or [Fulbright](https://us.fulbrightonline.org), after which we can help you find affordable student housing. Please also see the NTNU page about [placement students](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/placement) before sending me an email.

## Master's Thesis

I am happy to supervise master's students in the [Mathematical Sciences](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/msmnfma), [Cyber Security and Data Communication](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/mtkom), [Digital Infrastructure and Cyber Security](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/mstcnns), or [Security and Cloud Computing](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/mssecclo) programs at NTNU. Please get in touch if you are enrolled in one of these programs, have top grades in at least two of the courses [TTM4135 – Applied Cryptography and Network Security](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TTM4135), [TTM4138 – Wireless Network Security](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TTM4138), [TMA4160 – Cryptography](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TMA4160), [TMA4162 – Computational Algebra](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TMA4162), [TTM4195 – Blockchain Technologies and Cryptocurrencies](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TTM4195), or [TTM4205 – Secure Cryptographic Implementations](https://www.ntnu.edu/studies/courses/TTM4205), and are interested in writing a master's thesis in cryptography.


## PhD or Postdoc Positions

All my open positions are announced on [my homepage](/) and at [iacr.org/jobs](https://iacr.org/jobs). All applications must be submitted through [jobbnorge.no](https://www.jobbnorge.no/search/en) to be evaluated. I expect applicants to have a strong background in cryptography, and preferably also solid experience with either information security, algebra and number theory, algorithms and programming, theoretical computer science, or electrical engineering.

I like to work in teams, which is why most of my papers have three or more co-authors. I also like to meet regularly with my students, and you can expect a weekly one-hour meeting when we work together. When applying, make sure that your interests are aligned with [my research](/) and show that you have [read some of my work](/research/).
