I believe it is important to contribute to the research community: by organizing seminars and conferences where people can meet and discuss shared interests, and by serving on program committees to support the progress of open scientific research.

## Organization

I am organizing the [RESISTANT Symposium](https://www.ntnu.edu/iik/resistant) in Røros on November 23–26, 2026, together with [Poul Einar Heegaard](https://www.ntnu.edu/employees/poul.heegaard), [Stanislav Lange](https://www.ntnu.edu/employees/stanislav.lange), and [Hanno Langweg](https://www.ntnu.edu/employees/hanno.langweg).

I organized the [International Workshop on Foundations and Applications of Privacy-Enhancing Cryptography](https://privcryptworkshop.github.io), an affiliated event of [IACR Eurocrypt 2026](https://eurocrypt.iacr.org/2026) in Rome on May 10, 2026, together with [Lucjan Hanzlik](https://lucjanhanzlik.github.io) and [Daniel Slamanig](https://danielslamanig.info).

I was General Chair of [IACR PKC 2025](https://pkc.iacr.org/2025), held in [Røros](https://www.visitnorway.com/places-to-go/trondelag/roros) on May 12–15, 2025, together with [Bor de Kock](https://bordekock.nl).

I organized the workshop [Foundations and Applications of Zero-Knowledge Proofs](https://icms.ac.uk/activities/workshop/foundations-and-applications-of-zero-knowledge-proofs) at the International Centre for Mathematical Sciences in Edinburgh in 2024, together with [Elizabeth Crites](https://elizabeth-crites.github.io), [Markulf Kohlweiss](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/mkohlwei), and [Akira Takahashi](https://akiratk0355.github.io).

I was on the organizing committee of [IACR Eurocrypt 2022](https://eurocrypt.iacr.org/2022) in Trondheim, with [Colin Boyd](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=CIfBKmMAAAAJ) as General Chair.

I organized the [Norwegian Crypto Seminar 2020](https://wiki.math.ntnu.no/nks/nks20) with Kristian Gjøsteen and Martin Strand, the [Norwegian Crypto Seminar 2021](https://wiki.math.ntnu.no/nks/nks21) with Lise Millerjord and Martin Strand, and the [Norwegian Crypto Seminar 2024](https://wiki.math.ntnu.no/nks/nks24) with Slobodan Petrovic.

## External Assignments

I have testified as an expert witness on the encryption used in the end-to-end encrypted service SKY ECC, and on the data collection performed through the man-in-the-middle attack conducted by Europol against it, in 1) the [Borgarting Court of Appeal](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LBSTR/avgjorelse/lb-2024-142625) in Oslo in April 2025, 2) the Oslo District Court in May 2025, and 3) the [Gulating Court of Appeal](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LGSTR/avgjorelse/lg-2025-43360) in Bergen in June 2025.

I am a reviewer for the Systems and Electrical Engineering review panel at the Swedish Research Council.
